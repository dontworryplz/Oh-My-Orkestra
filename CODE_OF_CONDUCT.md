# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in Oh-My-Orkestra to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or the AI models they prefer. Disagreement about architecture is welcome; contempt for a person is not.

## Our Standards

Examples of expected behaviour:

- Critique the diff, the evidence, and the tradeoff — not the contributor.
- Assume a model, tool, or provider difference is a fact to document, not a side to win.
- Accept that "it works on my machine" is not evidence; help get a reproducible command.
- Respect that maintainers may reject a feature that adds scope, cost, or a second source of truth.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information, credentials, or logs without consent.
- Deliberately submitting secrets, prompt-injection payloads, or supply-chain risks.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the repository maintainer via a private channel (GitHub profile contact or a security advisory if the report involves credentials). Reports are reviewed confidentially. Consequences scale with severity: a private warning, removal from the conversation, or a permanent ban from the project.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed by other maintainers.
